Perth, WA

Rotorcross

35 Weston Road, Pickering Brook WA 6076

Established in 2014 by active members of the WA FPV and Multirotor communities, our home grounds in the Perth hills allows members to enjoy a safe, welcoming and approved space to fly till your hearts content. With features like no other club in Australia, such as permanent pilot pit, charging and spotter stations. We run midweek training sessions, monthly race meets, and casual/rookie days.

Club site Website Email
Kwinana, WA

West Coast Multirotor Club

Thomas Kelly Pavilion, Kwinana WA

Perth's FPV drone racing club, est. 2016. We run fortnightly race events throughout the year plus casual, beginner-friendly race and freestyle days. Our home base at Thomas Kelly Pavilion, Kwinana has shaded pits, power for charging LiPos, toilets, a canteen, a licensed sports bar and TV screens and ground stations to follow the action. Our mission is to promote the sport of FPV drone racing, foster innovation in drone technology and build a supportive community of pilots. Home of the 2026 FlyIQ WA State Drone Racing Championships.
